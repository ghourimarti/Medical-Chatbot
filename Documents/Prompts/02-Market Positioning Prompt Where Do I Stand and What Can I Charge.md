Deliver a **brutally honest, realistic assessment**. No motivational padding. No aspirational rate quoting. If a number is uncomfortable, give the uncomfortable number. I would rather hear it now than waste months bidding at the wrong level.

- **100% = expert** — someone who can independently architect, build, deploy, secure, observe, and operate enterprise-grade GenAI systems at multi-million-user scale, lead a team doing it, and pass interviews at FAANG-tier or top AI labs.
- **"Realistic"** = what would actually happen, accounting for: my Pakistan location, the absence of brand-name employer logos on my profile, the absence of production traffic experience, client country bias on freelance platforms, and the gap between *building portfolio projects* and *operating real production systems with real users and real on-call*.
